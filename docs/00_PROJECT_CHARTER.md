# 00 — Project Charter

## Mission
Build a public, independently created, synthetic prototype showing how a Controller can connect NetSuite inventory, physical operations, contracts, management review, and audit support into a traceable year-end inventory close.

## Company and period
Fictional growth-stage physical-AI company **KestrelGrid AI**. FY2026 year-end is December 31, 2026. Rough operating context: ~175 employees, ~600 customers, ~1,700 customer locations, U.S.-centric operations, centralized warehouse plus vendors/CMs/3PLs/installers/customer-site hardware.

## Product thesis
**The evidence layer between NetSuite inventory operations and the financial close.** The system should feel like a Controller workspace + evidence system + management workpaper platform, not a generic chatbot.

## Scope
Population, physical and cycle count, cutoff, goods in transit, ownership, demos/loaners/RMA, third party, valuation, GL reconciliation, proposed adjustments, PBC package, evidence lineage, assumptions, security, deterministic rules, replay, and Ask Gaurd.

## Locked architecture
Deterministic Core. Generative Edge. NetSuite read-only first. Financial Life of the Unit is flagship. NetSuite / Physical Reality / Accounting Evidence remain distinct. Native NetSuite controls remain distinct from close controls.

## Success
A sophisticated finance reviewer can see that unfamiliar complexity was structured into explicit facts, controls, evidence gaps, human judgment, and reproducible outputs without overclaiming expertise.

## Public safety
All data, firms, people, contracts, transaction identifiers, workpapers, and audit requests are synthetic. No real company references or proprietary data.
