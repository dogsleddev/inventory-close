# 01 — Product Principles

1. Evidence before inference.
2. AI investigates; Controllers conclude.
3. Operational data tells what happened; accounting evidence determines what belongs on the books.
4. Every material conclusion traces to evidence.
5. Human judgment is a control.
6. Location is not ownership.
7. Confidence is not certainty.
8. Audit readiness is not audit approval.
9. External auditors independently determine their procedures and samples.
10. Telemetry corroborates physical facts; it does not establish ownership, revenue, inventory relief, or lease classification by itself.
11. Third-party/offsite inventory is first class.
12. Valuation indicators can be deterministic; management concludes reserve treatment.
13. Detailed inventory must reconcile through subledger to GL; a forced zero difference is not enough.
14. Proposed JEs are prepared and reviewed; no autonomous posting.
15. Source lineage includes source system/record, retrieval, original/normalized value, transformation, reviewer, status, and retained artifact/hash.
16. Deterministic Core. Generative Edge.
17. The LLM may explain the control result; it may not create it.
18. Natural language cannot bypass controls.
19. Chat context is not accounting evidence.
20. One structured source of truth serves UI, Ask Gaurd, and future MCP.
21. Missing required evidence fails visibly.
22. Reproducibility is a control.
23. Generated language is not the system of record.
24. Demo reliability must not depend on live AI.
25. Extend NetSuite; do not rebuild it.
26. Native system control and close control answer different questions.
27. Financial Life of the Unit is a flagship feature.
28. Cycle-count history is a management risk lens, not auditor-sampling logic.
29. Transaction-chain completeness is evidence coverage, not confidence.
30. NetSuite integration is read-only first.
