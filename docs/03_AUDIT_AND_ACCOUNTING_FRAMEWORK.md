# 03 — Audit and Accounting Framework

Management owns the financial statements, accounting policy, population, reconciliations, conclusions, adjustments, workpapers, and responses to audit requests. The external auditor independently determines procedures, materiality, sampling, sufficiency of audit evidence, and audit conclusions.

## Assertions
Existence, Completeness, Rights & Obligations, Cutoff, Valuation, Classification; Accuracy is used for calculation/reconciliation/data issues.

Sheet→Floor principally supports Existence. Floor→Sheet principally supports Completeness.

## Core control domains
- Inventory population and subledger-to-GL reconciliation.
- Year-end physical count, recounts, movement control, and independent external-auditor selections.
- Inbound/outbound cutoff and goods in transit.
- Contractual ownership/acceptance; location alone is not ownership.
- Demo/loaner/customer-site/RMA/third-party inventory.
- Aging, excess, obsolete, damaged and RMA valuation review.
- Proposed adjustments, sign-off blockers, PBC preparation, evidence lineage and period lock.
- NetSuite three-way-match and sales-chain data as supporting/native controls, not substitutes for close conclusions.
- Historical NetSuite cycle counts as management evidence/risk signals, not automatic year-end conclusions or auditor reliance.

## Deterministic boundary
Rules detect structured conditions; management reaches controlled conclusions. AI retrieves/explains/drafts only. Required missing evidence remains unresolved.
